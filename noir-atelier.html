Fashion / Luxury — 2022

Noir Atelier

A luxury fashion house digital presence with editorial-grade visuals and smooth animations.

Challenge

Noir Atelier, a heritage fashion house, struggled to translate their physical atelier experience into the digital realm. Their existing website felt outdated and failed to capture the craftsmanship and artistry that defined their brand across generations.

Strategy

We treated the website as a digital fashion magazine — editorial, immersive, and deeply aesthetic. The strategy focused on visual storytelling, with each collection presented as a curated editorial spread rather than a product catalog.

Design

We developed a refined aesthetic with dramatic typography, generous whitespace, and cinematic motion. Custom cursor interactions, image reveal animations, and a navigation that feels more like flipping through a high-end fashion publication.

Development

Built with a focus on performance and visual fidelity. Implemented advanced image lazy loading, smooth page transitions, and custom WebGL effects for product showcases. The site achieved perfect Lighthouse scores despite heavy visual content.

Result

Noir Atelier received critical acclaim from Vogue, Harper's Bazaar, and Dezeen. Online inquiries increased by 200%, and the brand successfully attracted a younger demographic while maintaining their traditional clientele. The site became a benchmark for luxury fashion web design.
