Hospitality / Brand — 2023

Obsidian Hotels

Luxury hotel booking experience with cinematic storytelling and immersive room previews.

Challenge

Obsidian Hotels had a functional booking engine but lacked the emotional resonance expected of a luxury brand. Their website felt transactional, missing the opportunity to sell the dream of the destination before the guest ever arrived.

Strategy

We transformed the booking process into a journey. Each property page became a cinematic experience with drone footage, 360° room tours, and personalized recommendations. The booking engine was redesigned to feel like a concierge service, not a form.

Design

We created a visual language inspired by the properties themselves — dark, warm, and sophisticated. Large-format photography, smooth parallax scrolling, and an intuitive booking widget that feels integrated rather than intrusive.

Development

Built on a headless CMS for flexible content management. Implemented 360° room viewers, dynamic pricing engines, and a custom booking API. Performance optimized to handle high-resolution media without compromising load times.

Result

Obsidian Hotels saw a 75% increase in direct bookings, a 50% reduction in booking abandonment, and a 90% increase in time-on-site. The platform was awarded "Best Hotel Website" at the Luxury Travel Awards.
