Vertex Dynamics

An AI-powered analytics platform with real-time data streams and predictive modeling.

Challenge

Vertex Dynamics had powerful AI models but struggled to present them in an accessible way. Their existing platform was built for data scientists, not business users. They needed to democratize their AI without diluting its power.

Strategy

We created a dual-interface system: an expert mode for data scientists and an intuitive dashboard for business stakeholders. Natural language querying allowed users to ask questions in plain English and receive visual answers instantly.

Design

We designed a futuristic yet clean interface with a dark theme optimized for long data sessions. Custom visualization components, progressive disclosure of complexity, and a design system that could grow with the product roadmap.

Development

Built with React and TensorFlow.js for client-side ML inference. WebSocket connections for real-time data streaming, Canvas API for custom charts, and a microservices backend handling model training and inference at scale.

Result

Vertex Dynamics achieved 500% growth in enterprise accounts, reduced time-to-insight from hours to seconds, and was featured in TechCrunch for "making AI accessible to the enterprise." The platform now processes over 1 million predictions daily.
