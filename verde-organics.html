Lifestyle / D2C — 2023

Verde Organics

A sustainable lifestyle brand with an immersive product experience and subscription flows.

Challenge

Verde Organics had a strong product but a weak digital presence. Their existing Shopify store felt generic, and their subscription model was difficult to manage. They needed an experience that reflected their commitment to sustainability and quality.

Strategy

We built a custom Shopify Plus storefront with a focus on storytelling. Every product page became an editorial experience, with sustainability metrics, ingredient sourcing stories, and a seamless subscription management system.

Design

We crafted a clean, organic aesthetic with earthy tones and botanical typography. Interactive product tours, animated ingredient origins on a map, and a subscription dashboard that makes managing deliveries effortless.

Development

Custom Shopify theme with Hydrogen for headless capabilities. Integrated subscription APIs, carbon footprint calculator, and a loyalty program engine. Optimized for Core Web Vitals and accessibility.

Result

Verde Organics achieved 180% increase in online revenue, 65% increase in subscription retention, and a 4.8/5 average product rating. The brand was highlighted by Shopify as a "remarkable example of sustainable e-commerce."
